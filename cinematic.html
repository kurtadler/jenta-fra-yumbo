The author

Kurt Adler Dueland Juvik

Kurt Adler Dueland Juvik is a Norwegian author from Askøy near Bergen. In 2002, he survived a life-threatening accident that changed his life. At the time, no one could have imagined how profoundly that day would shape the years to come.

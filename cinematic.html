The story

A day that would change more than one life

On 7 December 2002, a life changed forever. But what happened that day was only the beginning.

No one could have imagined what the future had in store.

The dreams

One night, Hans had a dream he would never forget.

Hans heard a voice, but could not see who was speaking. The voice spoke three words:

«Ya es tiempo.»

Only later would he begin to understand.

Another night, a man stood before Hans.

Could this be the man whose voice he had heard in the first dream?

Hans noticed the wounds in the man's hands.

He was filled with overwhelming gratitude.

Hans spoke in a voice that came from deep within him.

Thank you for what you did for all of us.

The man did not answer.

A voice

Her voice made them feel truly present.

Some voices are never forgotten

Beyond the moment

Perhaps one act of kindness is all it takes to make a difference.

A story inspired by true events about what can happen when we see another person.

The author

Kurt Adler Dueland Juvik

Kurt Adler Dueland Juvik is a Norwegian author from Askøy near Bergen. In 2002, he survived a life-threatening accident that changed his life. At the time, no one could have imagined how profoundly that day would shape the years to come.

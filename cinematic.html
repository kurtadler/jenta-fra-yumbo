The story

A day that would change more than one life

On 7 December 2002, something happened that set a series of unexpected events in motion. No one could have foreseen where it would lead, or how people living thousands of miles apart would one day become part of the same story.

Was it coincidence? Was it fate? Or was it something that could only be understood much later? Sometimes, the meaning of a moment is hidden in everything that follows.

A voice

Beyond the moment

What if one act of kindness could change everything?

The author

Kurt Adler Dueland Juvik

Kurt Adler Dueland Juvik is a Norwegian author from Askøy near Bergen. In 2002, he was involved in a serious accident that changed his life. At the time, no one could have imagined the significance this event would later have.
